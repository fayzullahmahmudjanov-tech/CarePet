    Frequently asked questions

    Can I switch plans later?

    Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately and we prorate the difference.

    What if my pet needs emergency care?

    Emergency visits are included in the Elite plan. Essential and Complete Protection plans receive discounted emergency rates.

    Do plans cover multiple pets?

    Each plan covers one pet. Additional pets receive a 15% discount on their plan subscription.

    Are pre-existing conditions covered?

    Our plans focus on preventive and routine care. We treat pre-existing conditions at standard rates, regardless of plan membership.
